Shubhendu Jena

Research Engineer in 3D Computer Vision

I am a Research Engineer at CEA List, working on multi-camera 3D perception for autonomous driving. I received my PhD in Computer Science from Inria / Université de Rennes, where I worked on implicit and explicit representations for 3D shape and appearance.

My research interests include sparse-view 3D reconstruction, Gaussian splatting, neural rendering, novel-view synthesis, and bird’s-eye-view perception.
