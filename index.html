Fast, accurate 3D from less input.

Sparfels

Fast Reconstruction from Sparse Unposed Imagery

Turns as few as three uncalibrated views into detailed geometry in under three minutes on a consumer GPU. The pipeline combines MASt3R initialization, bundle-adjusting 2D Gaussian Splatting, correspondence-guided camera optimization, and a new ray-wise color-variance objective.

SparSplat

Generalizable 2D Gaussian Splatting

A feed-forward system for sparse-view reconstruction and novel-view synthesis. Multi-view foundation features are warped into the target view, then decoded into perspective-accurate surface elements for real-time rendering and mesh extraction.

State of the art on sparse DTU reconstruction, with nearly two orders of magnitude faster inference than prior volume-rendering methods.
